# New listings: are a market's first prices wrong?

argus-newlistings session, 2026-10-22/23. Code: `argus/newlistings/`. Tests: `tests/test_newlistings.py` (18).
Backtest and live rows: `newlistings_log.csv` (on the board). Full tables, regenerated by
`python -m argus.newlistings report`: `data/cache/newlistings/report.md` (not committed, it's a cache).

**Verdict: no variant passes.** New markets do start with worse prices than they end with, but mostly
because they have no real price at all, and where they do, the error is too small and too noisy to beat
the fee with a rule known at the time. One rule (fade early prices by price band) is positive but not
proven; it is running as a live paper test.

## What was measured

- **Sample.** 3,512 settled markets, drawn at random (fixed seed, blind to price and result) from the same
  927 series the favorites backtest used (60 per category). Up to 8 events per category per week by
  open date, and 3 markets per event. Opened 2025-04-01 to 2026-10-20, lived 48 hours or more. No
  15-minute markets, no combos. 18 categories; mostly Sports (935), Mentions (686), Commodities (519).
- **Prices.** Hourly candles at 1h, 6h and 24h after `open_time`, and at 24h before `close_time`. Only a
  candle that ended at or before that moment counts. A price counts only with a real book (bid within
  5c of the ask); the "price" is the Yes mid.
- **Early closes** (600 markets that closed well before their series normally does, i.e. "it happened")
  are left out of the calibration tables, because "24h before close" looks into the future there.
- **Trades.** For markets with a real book at 1h or 24h, the trade tape for the first 25 hours, for the
  maker fill test.

## 1. How often is there even a price?

| Moment | Real two-sided book |
|---|---|
| 1h after open | 13% |
| 6h after open | 25% |
| 24h after open | 36% |
| 24h before close | 39% |

Most new listings have an empty book (1c/99c) for their first day. "Early quoters set bad prices" mostly
means "nobody quotes yet", and an empty book can't be traded against.

## 2. Where early prices are most wrong

Same markets, early price against the 24h-before-close price and against the outcome. Bias = average
early Yes mid minus the share that won (positive = Yes priced too high). Ranges are 95%, resampling whole
events.

| Slice | n (events) | Bias at that moment | Brier early | Brier late |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All, 1h after open | 231 (139) | +1.5 pts (-3..+7) | 0.154 | 0.097 |
| All, 24h after open | 640 (377) | **+5.3 pts (+2..+8)** | 0.171 | 0.133 |
| Commodities, 24h | 149 (89) | +6.7 (-0..+14) | 0.165 | 0.071 |
| Sports, 24h | 285 (154) | +1.0 (-2..+4) | 0.189 | 0.189 |
| Mentions, 24h | 98 (64) | +7.7 (-1..+16) | 0.150 | 0.132 |
| Band 50-70c, 24h | 102 (97) | **+12.3 (+3..+22)** | 0.258 | 0.195 |
| Band 15-30c, 24h | 120 (108) | +6.7 (+1..+13) | 0.138 | 0.125 |
| Band 70-85c, 24h | 73 (70) | +9.8 (-1..+21) | 0.226 | 0.131 |
| Events with 16+ strikes, 24h | 213 (131) | +7.3 (+1..+13) | 0.158 | 0.087 |
| Events with 2-5 strikes, 24h | 262 (137) | +2.6 (-1..+6) | 0.196 | 0.185 |
| Numeric ladders, 1h | 173 (98) | +2.6 (-3..+9) | 0.155 | 0.087 |
| Pick-one lists, 1h | 55 (38) | -5.2 (-13..+1) | 0.132 | 0.121 |

How to read it:
- The big Brier gap (early worse than late) is almost all **Commodities and big numeric ladders**. That is
  news arriving (the oil price moves over the market's life), not early quoters being wrong. Sports
  early and late prices are equally good (gap +0.000).
- The tradable signal is the bias: 24h after open, Yes is priced about 5 points too high, most in the
  50-85c band and in big ladders. But the late price is also too high (late 0.370 vs 0.342 won), so part
  of this is Kalshi's general Yes overpricing that the maker/longshot work found, not something special to
  new listings.
- 1h after open there are too few real books (231) to say anything by slice.

## 3. Strategies (walk-forward, no look-ahead)

Every rule learns only from markets that **settled before** the entry moment. A cell's bias is trusted
once it has 40+ settled markets from 15+ events. Model Yes = current mid + the cell's past bias. Bet the
side with 3+ points of edge after the fee. Taker variants pay the ask and the taker fee. Maker variants
rest at bid+1c for 24 hours and count only if a later taker trade crossed the limit (maker fee only where
the series charges it). Every bet is held to settlement. Halves split by entry time; "recent" = entries
from 2026-07-20.

| Variant (engine `newlisting-…`) | n (events) | ROI after fees (95% event range) | 1st half | 2nd half | Recent 3 mo | Brier model / price | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| wf-1h (category x band, taker) | 0 | - | - | - | - | - | never qualified (too few real books) |
| wf-6h | 11 (11) | +2.5% (-25..+20) | +19% | -13% | +2.5% | 0.092 / 0.094 | TOO EARLY |
| wf-24h | 37 (37) | -3.1% (-30..+23) | +2.7% | -8.3% | +1.9% | 0.231 / 0.228 | NO EDGE |
| **wfband-1h** (band pooled, taker) | 24 (22) | +11.0% (-17..+39) | +23% | -3.8% | +11.0% | 0.191 / 0.199 | positive, not proven |
| **wfband-6h** | 92 (75) | +9.9% (-5..+25) | +5.5% | +15.1% | +9.9% | 0.182 / 0.183 | positive, not proven |
| **wfband-24h** | 293 (229) | +4.8% (-5..+14) | +8.5% | +0.7% | **-2.8%** | 0.186 / 0.187 | positive, not proven |
| mk-1h (rule side, maker) | 0 | - | - | - | - | - | never qualified |
| mk-6h | 5 (5) | +4.2% (-55..+27) | +24% | -21% | +4.2% | 0.061 / 0.068 | TOO EARLY |
| mk-24h | 12 (9) | -23.8% (-82..+48) | 0% | -56% | -51% | 0.239 / 0.216 | TOO EARLY |
| mkboth-1h (rest both sides at listing) | 255 filled (144) | -3.9% (-12..+3) | -6.8% | -1.3% | +0.3% | = price | NO EDGE |
| mkboth-1h, strict fills | 213 (130) | -6.3% (-15..+3) | -11.7% | -1.4% | +4.8% | = price | NO EDGE |
| mkboth-24h | 202 filled (126) | -2.3% (-11..+7) | -8.8% | +4.5% | +4.8% | = price | NO EDGE |

Maker fill rates: at 1h, 745 possible resting orders (both sides of every real book), 255 filled (34%);
at 24h, 1,513 possible, 202 filled (13%). The filled ones lose about what the spread gives: adverse
selection.

The wfband rule almost always bets **No** (the band bias is "Yes too high"); its biggest groups are
Commodities, Sports, Mentions and Politics at 24h. No range clears zero. wfband-24h, the one with real
numbers (229 events), went from +8.5% in the first half to +0.7% in the second half and -2.8% in the last
three months. That is what a fading edge or a lucky start looks like. wfband-6h is the most promising
(both halves positive, model Brier just under the price's) but its range is -5% to +25% on 75 events.

## 4. How many listings qualify per week

- In the sample: wfband-24h 3.6 bets/week, wfband-6h 1.1, wfband-1h 0.3, maker-both 2.5-3.2 filled/week.
  The sample is about 1 in 6 of in-scope markets in 60 series per category, so this is far below the
  market-wide number.
- Kalshi-wide: at least 71,700 non-combo, 48h+ markets opened in the last 7 days (63,622 Sports; the
  count hit the 400-page cap, so it's a lower bound). Scaling each category's recent-3-month rate gives
  about **5,700/week for wfband-6h and 8,200/week for wfband-24h**. That's a rough guess driven by Sports.
- Measured: one live run on 2026-10-23 16:42 UTC (markets aged 6-8h and 24-26h) found **84 qualifying
  calls on 62 events** (54 at 6h, 30 at 24h; 69 Sports, 12 Commodities, 3 Economics), after a cap of 3
  per event. Volume is not the problem. A real edge is.

## 5. The live command

```
python -m argus.newlistings live            # 6h and 24h rules, markets aged h to h+2 hours, logs paper calls
python -m argus.newlistings live --no-log   # just list them
python -m argus.newlistings settle          # fill results for pending live rows
```

Live rows are engine `newlisting-live-wfband-6h` / `-24h`, 1 paper contract at the ask, taker fee stored
on the row. The cell bias comes from all 3,512 settled sample markets. Run it every 2 hours or so (the
window is 2 hours; `--window` changes it).

## 6. Caveats

- The candle mid is an hourly close, not the exact book at the moment. A real taker at the ask may get less
  size than one contract's worth of signal suggests.
- Maker fills ignore queue position and partial fills. We assume our bid+1c made a new best price, so
  we were first at that level. The strict version (trade strictly through the limit) is worse, not better.
- Kalshi returned 429 on about 40% of requests during collection (several sessions share the limit).
  Every call is retried and logged (`data/cache/newlistings/api_calls.csv`); nothing was skipped
  (0 errors).
- Cells were chosen before the results (category x band, then band pooled). The 3-point edge threshold
  and the 40-market minimum were not tuned.
